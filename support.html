Support & FAQ

What is Campus SnapLock?

We are a platform for sharing and accessing digital campus content like notes, guides, and media.

How do I access locked content?

Click the content card, review the price, and complete the secure payment via Mobile Money or Card. Access is granted instantly upon successful payment.

What happens after I pay?

You will receive an instant digital delivery of the content or a secure access code, depending on the item type.

How do I provide feedback?

We value user input! Send your suggestions or feedback to support@kford-digitals.xyz.
